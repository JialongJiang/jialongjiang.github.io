Single-cell RNA sequencing across 40 signaling conditions and D-SPIN regulatory
network models reveal how combinations of signals tune the proportions of neurons
and glia. The study identifies a candidate noise-driven differentiation switch
that links transcriptional variability to probabilistic cell-fate decisions.
